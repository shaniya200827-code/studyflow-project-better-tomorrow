The Student Challenge

Studying shouldn't feel chaotic.

College life involves competing assignments, sudden assessments, and fragmented hours. Students often struggle to manage multiple priorities without burning out.

📚

Too many subjects & deadlines

Balancing five or six demanding courses at once makes it difficult to gauge which deadline needs immediate attention and which can wait.

⚖️

Deciding what to study first

Decision paralysis consumes valuable study energy. Students often spend hours wondering where to begin instead of actually learning.

📉

Lack of progress tracking

Without visible metrics, it is easy to feel unaccomplished or lose momentum. Traditional to-do lists don't connect daily effort to exam readiness.

Core Features

Designed for Student Success

Everything you need to master your academic timetable without cognitive overload.

🧭

Smart Planning

Create a customized study schedule based on your specific subjects, exam dates, difficulty levels, and available daily hours.

🎯

Priority-Based Scheduling

Our rule-based scheduler allocates greater attention and prime focus blocks to subjects that are more urgent or demanding.

⏱️

Focus Mode

Eliminate distractions with a dedicated Pomodoro countdown timer with switchable 25, 45, and 60-minute deep-work intervals.

Track completed tasks, subject-by-subject percentage completion, total study minutes, and consecutive day streaks.

Design Thinking Context

Built for Better Tomorrows

StudyFlow was created as a Design Thinking prototype to address the everyday friction students face when managing study time, deadlines, and competing academic priorities.

Design Thinking Methodology

This prototype demonstrates the complete 5-stage human-centered design approach:

1. Empathize & Define

Interviewing peers revealed overwhelming cognitive load when trying to schedule multi-subject revision.

2. Ideation & Prototype

Formulated a transparent rule-based algorithm linking urgency, difficulty, and available daily hours.

3. Validation & Testing

Structured testing cycles with college peers to measure usability, clarity, and genuine study completion.

Prototype Note: StudyFlow is a functional college prototype created for usability testing. All priority calculations are transparent and deterministic, with zero external server dependencies.
